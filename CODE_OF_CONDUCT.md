# Code of Conduct

We are committed to providing a friendly, safe, and welcoming environment for all contributors.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful and inclusive in communication
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users
- Showing empathy towards others

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, discrimination, or intimidation of any kind
- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, or personal attacks
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this code of conduct. Serious or repeated violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from the project.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please contact the maintainers at support@glitech.cn.
